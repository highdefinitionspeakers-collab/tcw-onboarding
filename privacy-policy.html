Privacy Policy

How Essential Distribution LLC and The Corporate Way collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you submit an intake form, enroll in a service tier, or interact with our client portal, we collect information you provide directly, including your name, phone number, email address, business name, business details, and any information you choose to share about your goals or challenges.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to evaluate your business needs, contact you about our services, process enrollment and payment, deliver the services you've purchased, and provide account updates through your client portal.

SMS & Text Communications

If you provide a phone number, you may receive SMS/text communications from us regarding your inquiry, enrollment, or account status. See our SMS Terms & Privacy page for full details on messaging frequency, opt-out, and carrier disclosures.

Information Sharing
  • We do not sell your data. Essential Distribution LLC does not sell personal information to third parties.
  • Service providers. We may share information with the tools and vendors that operate our business — for example, payment processing (Square), workflow automation (Zapier), and record-keeping (Airtable) — solely to deliver the services you've requested.
  • Referral partners. If you choose to engage with an optional referral partner (such as our credit consultation partner), your click or interaction may be shared with that partner. This is always your choice and never automatic.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.
Data Retention & Security

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and maintain business records, including compliance and tax obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. You can request that we update or delete your information by contacting us directly using the details below.
